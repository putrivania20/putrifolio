Marketing Campaign Analysis

Multi-channel marketing analysis optimizing ROI across 24 campaigns

Marketing Analytics Power BI Analytics Insights

Project Overview

This analysis examined 24 marketing campaigns across email, social media, paid search, and display advertising channels. Detailed ROI calculations helped identify the most effective channels and strategies for budget optimization.

The comprehensive analysis provided actionable recommendations that improved marketing efficiency and increased conversion rates significantly.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Attribution Complexity

Multi-touch attribution across multiple channels was difficult to track.

Solution: Implemented cross-channel attribution model using weighted touch-point analysis.

Challenge: Data Integration

Campaign data was scattered across multiple marketing platforms.

Solution: Created centralized Power BI dashboard pulling data from all channels.

Challenge: Seasonality Effects

Seasonal variations made performance comparisons difficult.

Solution: Applied seasonality adjustments and controlled for market factors.

Key Features

  • Multi-channel ROI Attribution

    Track return on investment across email, social, search, display

  • Campaign Performance Benchmarking

    Compare campaigns against industry standards and historical performance

  • Customer Acquisition Cost

    Detailed CAC analysis by channel and campaign

  • Lifetime Value Calculations

    Understand true value of customers acquired through each channel

  • A/B Testing Analysis

    Statistical significance testing for campaign variations

  • Optimization Recommendations

    Data-driven budget allocation suggestions
